# 🛒 Uncovering Retail Sales Trends
<br>
## 👋 The Project
<br>
Every transaction tells a story about who is buying, what they are buying, and when they are buying it. For this project, I stepped into the shoes of a retail data analyst to dissect a comprehensive retail sales dataset. 
<br>
My goal was to take raw transaction logs and turn them into actionable business intelligence. By cleaning the data and building pivot tables, I explored purchasing patterns, customer demographics, and seasonal sales trends.
<br>
## 🛠️ How I Built This
<br>
This project was driven entirely by **Microsoft Excel**. Here is my workflow:
<br>
* **Data Wrangling:** I took the raw dataset, standardized the formatting, checked for inconsistencies, and prepped it for analysis.
<br>
* **Aggregating the Data:** I utilized Excel Pivot Tables to slice the data by various dimensions—like age, gender, product category, and date.
<br>
* **Extracting Insights:** I focused on finding the "so what?" behind the numbers to help a hypothetical business optimize its inventory and marketing.
